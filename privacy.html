Privacy Policy: OpenAPI Swagger Viewer for Confluence

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]
Vendor: [VENDOR LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], [JURISDICTION] ("we", "us")
Contact for privacy questions and security reports: support@capella.business

1. The short version

  • The app shows OpenAPI/Swagger specs inside Confluence Cloud. It runs on Atlassian's Forge platform and is designed to Run on Atlassian: it makes no outbound network requests and declares no external permissions.
  • Your specs, page content and attachments are read in your own browser from your own Confluence site. They are not sent to us, and not to anyone else.
  • We do not store passwords, API tokens or any other credentials. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools inside the app.
  • The only app data we or the app keep is: the macro settings you enter, and small anonymous error counters, both held in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site.
  • We receive the usual customer and licence information from Atlassian Marketplace (who bought what, for which site), and the emails you send us for support.
  • We have no sub-processors. Atlassian hosts the app and its data.

2. Who is responsible for what

  • Your content (specs, page text, attachments, macro settings): you are the controller of this data. Because it stays on Atlassian's infrastructure and never reaches our own systems, we do not hold a copy. To the limited extent that running the app processes it on your behalf, we act as your processor and will sign a data processing agreement on request (support@capella.business).
  • Business and support data (Marketplace licence information, support emails): we are the controller.
  • Atlassian operates Confluence, Forge hosting and storage, and Marketplace billing. Atlassian's own privacy policy and terms apply to that processing: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

3.3 What we do not collect

  • Passwords, API tokens, OAuth tokens or any credentials (your CI pipeline uses your own Confluence token against Atlassian directly; it never passes through the app or through us).
  • The content of your pages, spreadsheets, specs or attachments.
  • Analytics, usage tracking, fingerprinting, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking scripts. The app sets no cookies.
  • Sensitive categories of personal data. Please do not put personal data into specs; they are meant to describe APIs.

4. How we use data

To run, secure, support and bill the app; to fix errors; to meet legal obligations; to communicate about outages, security issues and changes to these terms. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models.

8. Uninstalling and deleting data

  • If you uninstall the app, Atlassian soft-deletes the app's Forge storage for your site and permanently removes it after Atlassian's retention period. Atlassian's current documentation describes this period as 28 days after uninstall, with the possibility to relink data if you reinstall within 21 days. We cannot shorten or extend it.
  • Macro settings that sit inside Confluence pages stay in those pages until you delete the macro or the page. They belong to your Confluence content.
  • Attachments belong to your pages and are not removed by uninstalling the app.
  • To delete licence or support data we hold about you, email support@capella.business. We will do it unless the law requires us to keep it (for example invoice records), and tell you which parts we kept and why.

If you are a customer and your own users or data subjects contact us about content in your Confluence site, we will direct them to you, because you are the controller of that content and we do not hold it.
