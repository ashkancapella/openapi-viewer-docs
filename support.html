What to include in a bug report

  • Your Confluence site URL and the URL of the page with the macro
  • The OpenAPI version of your spec (2.0, 3.0.x or 3.1) and your browser
  • Whether you use paste or attachment mode
  • If you can share it, a minimal anonymised spec that reproduces the problem

Never send secrets

Do not send API tokens, passwords or confidential specs by email. If you need us to look at a spec, send a minimal anonymised example. If a token reaches us by mistake, revoke it right away.
